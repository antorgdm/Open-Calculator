# Privacy Policy

**Open Calculator** — operated by SE7EN ("we", "us", the "Service Provider")  
**Effective date:** 2026-10-15

## Summary
Open Calculator works entirely on your device. We do not collect, store on a server, sell, share or transmit any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no internet permission.

## What the app stores on your device
The following is saved locally on your phone only, so the app can work, and never leaves it:
- Your calculation history (expressions, results, favorites, timestamps)
- Your settings (theme, language, numeral style, decimal precision, angle mode, sound and vibration choices)
- Memory and variable values used by the calculator

You can delete history at any time inside the app (History screen). Uninstalling the app removes all of this data. App data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

## Information we do not collect
We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, IP address, usage statistics or any other personal data. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels or advertising identifiers.

## Permissions
The app requests only the **Vibrate** permission, used for optional haptic feedback on key presses. It does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, storage or contacts permissions.

## Clipboard
The Copy and Paste buttons use the system clipboard only when you tap them. Clipboard contents are never read in the background or sent anywhere.

## Third parties
The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and shares no data with third parties.

## Children
The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your rights
Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control all locally stored data directly in the app or by uninstalling it.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted with a new effective date. Previous versions are available on request.
